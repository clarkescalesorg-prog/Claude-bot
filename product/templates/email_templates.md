# Email Templates

Plain-text only. No HTML, no tracking pixels on cold email — both kill
deliverability and trust. Send from a domain that has SPF, DKIM, and
DMARC configured, and never from your primary domain.

---

## Cold email — Warm tier — Day 0

**Subject:** Quick question about {business_name}

```
Hi {name},

Saw {business_name} on Google in {city} — {review_count} reviews and a
4.{x}-star average is a solid base.

Quick question: are you turning roofing jobs away at the moment, or could
you take on 4–6 more surveys a week if they came pre-qualified?

We work with one roofer per postcode area on a pay-per-booked-survey
basis. The {city} slot is currently open.

Worth a 10-minute call this week to see if it's a fit?

{sender_name}
{sender_phone}

To stop receiving emails from me, reply with "unsubscribe" and I'll
remove you within 24 hours.
```

## Cold email — Day 4 follow-up

**Subject:** Re: Quick question about {business_name}

```
Hi {name},

Bumping this in case it got buried. Happy to send through last month's
numbers from the roofer we work with in {nearby_city} — they took on 11
booked surveys for £440 total.

Want me to send them over?

{sender_name}
```

## Cold email — Day 11 break-up

**Subject:** Closing the file

```
Hi {name},

Last one from me — I'll assume the timing isn't right and close the file
on this. If anything changes, my number's below.

All the best with {business_name}.

{sender_name}
{sender_phone}
```

## Reply email — "Send more info"

**Subject:** Re: Quick question about {business_name}

```
Hi {name},

Short version:

- We get survey requests from homeowners in {city} via Google Ads.
- We pre-qualify them by phone (budget, timing, decision-maker).
- We pass them to one roofer per postcode — that's you, if it's a fit.
- You pay £40 per booked survey, nothing if it doesn't book.
- 14-day notice to cancel, no contract.

Want me to queue you for the next one as a no-cost trial?

{sender_name}
```

## Reply email — Pricing pushback

**Subject:** Re: Quick question about {business_name}

```
Hi {name},

Fair — £40/survey only makes sense if your average job value clears
£1,500 and your survey-to-job rate is above 40%. If either of those is
lower for {business_name}, this isn't a fit and I'd say so.

What's a typical job value for you in {city}?

{sender_name}
```
