## The problem

You currently rely on word of mouth and {existing_channel} to fill your
diary. That gives you {current_jobs_per_month} jobs a month at an average
value of £{avg_job_value}. You've told me you could take on
{spare_capacity} more jobs a month without hiring.

## The proposal

I'll deliver pre-qualified roofing survey requests to {business_name},
exclusive to you in the {postcode_area} area, on a pay-per-booked-survey
basis.

### What "pre-qualified" means

Each lead has confirmed:

1. The job is within your service area.
2. They own the property (not renting).
3. They have a budget of at least £{min_budget}.
4. They want a survey within the next 30 days.
5. They've agreed to a 30-minute window for your call.

### Volume

I aim to deliver **{target_leads_per_month} booked surveys per month**.
Some months will exceed this, some will fall short. I won't deliver
more than 1.5x target without your approval — capacity matters.

## Pricing

| Item | Price |
|------|-------|
| Setup | £0 |
| Per booked survey (first 10) | £{tier1_price} |
| Per booked survey (11+) | £{tier2_price} |
| Monthly minimum | £0 |

You only pay for surveys that actually take place. No-shows are free.
Cancellations within 24 hours of booking are free.

## Term

Month-to-month. Either side can end with 14 days' written notice. No
auto-renewal, no minimum spend, no exit fee.

## Exclusivity

For the duration of this agreement, I won't send leads from
{postcode_area} to any other roofing business. This is the core of why
the per-lead price is what it is.

## What I need from you

1. A direct mobile number for me to forward leads to.
2. A 5-minute reply SLA during business hours (Mon–Fri 8am–6pm).
3. Honest reporting of which leads booked and which didn't — I can't
   tune the system without it.

## What's not included

- Branding or website work.
- Quoting on jobs (you handle that).
- Insurance, certifications, or compliance for the work itself.

## Acceptance

Reply to the email this is attached to with **"Yes, let's start"** and
I'll send the first survey request within 5 working days.
