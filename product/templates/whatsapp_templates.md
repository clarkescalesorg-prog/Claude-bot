# WhatsApp Templates

WhatsApp Business outbound messages must be **pre-approved templates** if
the recipient hasn't messaged you in the last 24 hours. The text below is
written to pass Meta's template approval — no marketing taglines, clear
purpose, named placeholders.

Submit each one in WhatsApp Manager under **Marketing** category unless
noted.

---

## Template: `roofer_intro_warm` (Marketing)

```
Hi {{1}}, this is {{2}} from {{3}}. We send pre-qualified roofing survey
requests to one roofer per postcode area. The {{4}} slot is currently
open. Would you like to see how it works? Reply STOP to opt out.
```

Variables:
- `{{1}}` — first name
- `{{2}}` — your name
- `{{3}}` — your company
- `{{4}}` — city / postcode area

## Template: `roofer_followup_3d` (Marketing)

```
Hi {{1}}, following up on my message about survey requests for {{2}}.
Happy to send a free trial lead — no commitment. Reply YES if useful.
Reply STOP to opt out.
```

## Template: `roofer_breakup_9d` (Marketing)

```
Hi {{1}}, last message from me on this. I'll close the file unless you'd
like me to keep your number on record. Reply KEEP to stay in touch, or
just ignore this. Reply STOP to opt out.
```

## Template: `survey_booking_confirm` (Utility)

```
Hi {{1}}, confirming your call with {{2}} at {{3}} on {{4}}. We'll call
{{5}}. Reply RESCHEDULE if anything changes.
```

Utility-category templates are cheaper per message and don't count toward
marketing limits.

## Template: `survey_reminder_same_day` (Utility)

```
Hi {{1}}, quick reminder — your call with {{2}} is at {{3}} today.
Reply if you need to move it.
```

## Free-form (within 24-hour window) examples

Once a recipient replies, you have a 24-hour window to send free-form
messages. Use these for objection handling.

### "How much does it cost?"

> Depends on volume — typically £40 per booked survey for the first 10,
> then £30 after that. Want me to send a one-pager?

### "Send info"

> Sure — short version: we send you survey requests from homeowners in
> your area, you only pay when one books in. No setup fee. Want me to
> queue you for the next one in {city}?

### "Already use Checkatrade / MyBuilder"

> Makes sense — most roofers we work with do too. The difference is these
> leads are exclusive to you, not bid on by 5 others. Worth a 10-min
> chat?

### "Not interested"

> No problem — I'll close the file. All the best with {business_name}.

### "STOP"

(No reply. Add to suppression list immediately.)
