# Letter — Council Tax band challenge (England)

Send to: the **Valuation Office Agency** (VOA) using the online form at gov.uk/challenge-council-tax-band, **or** by post to the address on your council tax bill if your case requires the formal "proposal" route (i.e. you're outside the 6-month informal-challenge window since moving in).

The VOA's online form has free-text boxes — paste in the relevant sections below. Send the same content as a letter (printed, signed, dated) if you're going by post. Keep a copy.

---

## Content to use

> Subject: Request for review of Council Tax band — [your full address, including postcode]
>
> To the Valuation Office Agency,
>
> I am writing to request a formal review of the Council Tax band for the property at the above address. The current band is **[Band X]**.
>
> ### Grounds for review
>
> **1. Comparable properties.** I have researched the Council Tax bands of [number] neighbouring properties of similar size, age, and type. The following properties are in a lower band:
>
> | Address | Band | Notes (size / type) |
> |---------|------|---------------------|
> | [address 1] | [Band Y] | [e.g. same row, same layout, identical floor area] |
> | [address 2] | [Band Y] | [...] |
> | [address 3] | [Band Y] | [...] |
> | [address 4] | [Band Y] | [...] |
>
> The comparable properties are materially equivalent to my own. I therefore have reasonable grounds to believe my property has been incorrectly placed in a higher band.
>
> **2. Estimated 1991 valuation.** Using historical price data (Nationwide House Price Index, applied backwards from a [year] sale price of £[amount]), the estimated open-market value of this property on 1 April 1991 was approximately £[amount]. This falls within the threshold for **Band [Z]** under the 1991 valuation tables.
>
> **3. [Optional — only if relevant]** The property has the following characteristics that may further support a lower band: [e.g. no off-street parking, no garage, smaller plot size than neighbouring homes, structural alterations since the original valuation, etc.].
>
> ### Request
>
> Please review the banding of this property. If the review confirms an over-banding, I would expect the corrected band to be applied with retrospective effect to **the date I became liable for council tax at this address ([date])**, and any overpayment to be refunded by my local billing authority.
>
> I have attached / can supply on request:
> - Screenshots of the comparable properties from gov.uk/council-tax-bands.
> - The 1991 valuation calculation.
> - Floorplan / EPC of my property (where available).
>
> Please confirm receipt and the expected review timescale.
>
> Yours faithfully,
>
> [Full name]
> [Address]
> [Phone] · [Email]
> [Date]

---

## Tips for the response

- The VOA will write back within ~2 months. Most replies are one of:
  1. **Band changed** — they instruct your council to refund and rebill. Allow another 4–6 weeks for the council to issue the refund.
  2. **Band confirmed** — review their reasoning. If they've used different comparables than you, **respond** with why your comparables are more relevant (same row, same era, same type beats "another property in the same postcode").
  3. **Band increased** — rare, but possible. If the VOA think your case shows the *whole street* should go up, they may issue a "List Alteration" affecting neighbours. This is why the neighbour-check has to be done first.

- If your formal challenge is rejected, you have **3 months** to appeal to the **Valuation Tribunal** (free, independent). Most appeals at this stage are won when the original VOA decision used poor comparables.

- Don't engage no-win-no-fee "council tax refund" companies — they take 35–50% of the refund and ongoing savings, for filling in this same letter.
