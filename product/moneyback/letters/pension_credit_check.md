# Pension Credit — quickest path to claiming

Pension Credit is the single most under-claimed benefit in the UK. The DWP estimates ~850,000 pensioner households who are entitled to it don't claim. Average award: ~£3,900/year, plus passport access to:

- Free TV Licence (over 75s)
- Cold Weather Payments (£25 per 7-day cold spell)
- Warm Home Discount (£150)
- Council Tax Reduction (often 100%)
- Help with NHS dental, glasses, hospital travel
- Housing Benefit (if renting)

If a parent, grandparent, or older relative is over State Pension age, **do this for them today**. It's a 30-minute phone call.

---

## Eligibility — quick check

You're likely eligible if **all** of:

- The applicant (or their partner) is over **State Pension age** (66 currently; rising).
- They live in the UK.
- Their **weekly income** (state pension + private pension + earnings + most other income, but **not** Attendance Allowance / PIP / DLA) is below:
  - **Single:** ~£218/week (~£11,343/yr)
  - **Couple:** ~£333/week (~£17,313/yr)

Use the calculator at **gov.uk/pension-credit-calculator** to confirm and estimate the award.

**Apply even if marginally over the threshold.** The "Savings Credit" element and additional amounts for severe disability, carers, and housing costs can lift many people back into eligibility. The calculator may underestimate.

---

## Capital limits

There is no upper savings limit — you can have any amount of savings and still apply. However:

- The first £10,000 is ignored.
- Above £10,000, every £500 of savings is treated as £1/week of "tariff income."
- So someone with £30,000 savings has £40/week of deemed income added to their actual income.

This is more generous than Universal Credit. Many pensioners assume they're disqualified by savings — they're not.

---

## Apply

**Phone is much faster than online for older claimants.**

> **Pension Credit claim line: 0800 99 1234**
> Monday–Friday, 8am–6pm.
> Have ready: NI numbers (claimant + partner), bank details, info about pension and other income, savings totals, and (if relevant) housing costs.

The call lasts 25–40 minutes. The agent fills in the form for you. No paperwork.

The claim can be **backdated by 3 months** — ask explicitly. ("I'd like to backdate this claim to the earliest possible date, please.")

---

## After the claim

- Decision arrives by post in 4–6 weeks.
- If awarded, payments are weekly or 4-weekly into the nominated bank account.
- The DWP automatically passes Pension Credit recipients to relevant other schemes (Warm Home Discount, Council Tax Reduction may need a separate council application).

---

## If refused

The refusal letter will include the calculation. Common errors:

- **Income mis-stated** — e.g. private pension shown gross when it should be net of tax, or savings tariff calculated wrongly.
- **Severe Disability Premium not applied** — if the claimant lives alone and gets PIP daily-living or Attendance Allowance, they qualify for an additional ~£82/week. This is often missed.
- **Carer Premium not applied** — if a partner cares for the claimant.

You have **1 month** to ask for a **Mandatory Reconsideration**. Phone the number on the refusal letter and request it. About 20% of MRs change the decision. After that, you can appeal to a tribunal — also free.

Get free help from:

- **Age UK Advice Line:** 0800 678 1602
- **Citizens Advice:** 0800 144 8848

Both will help you fill in forms, attend appeals, and check the calculation. Their PC success rate is materially higher than self-applications.
