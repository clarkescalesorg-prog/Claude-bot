# Changelog

All notable changes to the UK Roofer Outreach Pack will be documented here.

## [1.0.0] — 2026-04-28

Initial release.

### Added
- `playbook.md` — 40-page outreach playbook covering sourcing,
  segmentation, compliance, cadence, and scaling.
- `templates/sms_templates.md` — 10 SMS templates across hot, warm, and
  cold tiers, plus booking confirmations and no-show recovery.
- `templates/whatsapp_templates.md` — 5 Meta-approvable WhatsApp
  templates with variable mappings, plus free-form objection handlers.
- `templates/email_templates.md` — Cold email sequence with reply
  templates for the most common objections.
- `templates/proposal.md` — Fill-in-the-blanks pricing proposal.
- `landing.html` — Single-file static sales page (resellable under the
  agency exception).
- `LICENSE.txt` — Single-business commercial license with agency
  exception.
- `README.md` — Buyer-facing overview.
