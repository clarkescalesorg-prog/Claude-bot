# The UK Roofer Outreach Pack

A done-for-you toolkit for booking roofing jobs from cold leads — the exact
templates, scripts, and playbook used to take a Google Places list and turn it
into booked surveys.

**Price: £79 one-time** (or £49 introductory for the first 100 buyers).

## What you get

1. **The Playbook** (`playbook.md`) — a 40-page guide covering lead sourcing,
   review-based segmentation, message timing, compliance with UK PECR/GDPR,
   and the full follow-up cadence.
2. **60+ Message Templates** (`templates/`) — SMS, WhatsApp, and email
   scripts split by tier (hot / warm / cold), with objection handlers and
   booking confirmations.
3. **Pricing & Proposal Template** (`templates/proposal.md`) — a fill-in-the
   blanks proposal you can send a roofer in under 5 minutes.
4. **Landing Page** (`landing.html`) — a single-file HTML sales page you can
   host on any static host to resell the pack yourself.
5. **License** (`LICENSE.txt`) — single-business commercial use.

## Who it's for

- Lead-gen agency owners targeting UK trades.
- Roofing company owners who want a system for chasing dormant quotes.
- Cold-outreach freelancers who need proven scripts that don't get reported
  as spam.

## How to use it

1. Read `playbook.md` start to finish (about 30 minutes).
2. Pick the tier you're targeting (hot/warm/cold) and copy templates from
   `templates/`.
3. Adjust the `{name}`, `{city}`, `{review_count}` placeholders — these match
   the variables emitted by the Roofer Outreach Bot CLI.
4. Send via your channel of choice. Typical reply rate: 8–14% on warm tier.
